CPRIT selects OncoLight for a $1.8 million seed award

The Cancer Prevention and Research Institute of Texas selected OncoLight, Inc. for a Seed Award for Product Development Research to advance its surgical imaging platform for high-grade glioma. Pablo Valdés leads the project. The award amount is pending contract negotiation.

Academic research

November 2, 2026

UTMB and Rice researchers study two optical signals at the glioma margin

Fernando Chiwo and colleagues, including OncoLight’s four co-founders, published a study in Neuro-Oncology on combining fluorescence and diffuse reflectance spectroscopy. The academic team compared the optical measurements with histology to study how glioma tissue differs from the surrounding brain.
